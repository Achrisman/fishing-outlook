## Regs check (every session) — MODE-CRITICAL
Shore anglers get exemptions from seasonal groundfish closures that DO NOT apply from a boat. Boat mode: verify current depth constraints + season before planning. This is the sharpest shore/boat divergence in the whole system.

## Location formulas
- **Fort Baker / Cavallo Cliff (mode: shore)**: producer on squid drop shot, 3oz bank, Beef Stick; early→mid flood logged (3/20/26: 2 brown rockfish kept). Cast past the worst rock zone into cleaner bottom.
- **HMB nearshore (mode: boat)**: rockfish alongside salmon program; Jiggy Jigs 20–50g by depth.

## Snag minimization (rocky shore)
Circle hooks; 12–18" leaders; minimum lead to hold; 10–12lb mono sacrifice link above sinker.

## Gear map
Beef Stick + 25lb fluoro; squid; bank sinkers; Jiggy Jig ladder (boat vertical).
